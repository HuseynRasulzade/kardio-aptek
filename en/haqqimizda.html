About us

Kardio Aptek is a modern pharmacy focused on heart health and everyday wellbeing.

Who we are

A modern pharmacy that cares about heart health

Kardio Aptek opened in 2024 in Baku, near Qara Qarayev metro station. Our goal is to give every customer convenient, reliable and clear service.

For every product we offer, the composition, dosage, manufacturer and expiry date are clearly shown. Product pages on this site also include packaging photos and an ingredient table so you can decide with confidence.
