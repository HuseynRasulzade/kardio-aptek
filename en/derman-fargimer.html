About the product

Fargimer is a combined throat spray.

Areas of use

  • As an aid in infectious inflammatory diseases of the upper respiratory tract: tonsillitis, pharyngitis, laryngitis, aphonia, dysphonia
  • Oropharyngeal infections
  • Rash and ulcerative stomatitis
  • Oral thrush (candidiasis) in newborns
  • Gingivitis, periodontosis
  • Sore and itchy throat
  • Unpleasant breath
  • After surgery

Excipients

Propylene glycol, potassium sorbate, potassium iodide, distilled water.

Directions and dosage

  • Taken orally (see the packaging photos for the timing relative to meals).
  • 0–6 months: 1 dose 1–2 times a day.
  • 6–12 months: 1–2 doses 1–2 times a day.
  • 1–3 years: 2 doses 3 times a day.
  • 3–6 years: 2 doses 3–4 times a day.
  • 6 years and older: 1–2 doses 5–6 times a day, i.e. every 3–4 hours.
  • Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

Contraindications

  • No contraindications are listed on the label — consult a doctor before use.

Storage conditions

At 18–25 °C, away from direct heat and sunlight and out of reach of children.
