Depomer D3+K2

Recommended as an additional source of vitamins D3 and K2.

Dosage formOral solution (drops)
Packaging15 ml glass vial, cardboard box (1 ml = 25 drops)
Pharmacy dispensingOver the counter
Shelf life3 years

Ingredients

Composition per 15 ml (1 ml = 25 drops)
IngredientAmount
Vitamin D3Cholecalciferol50,000 IU
Vitamin K2Menaquinone200 mcg

Excipients

Olive oil.

Directions and dosage

  • Children 2–4 years: 1 drop once a day.
  • Children 4–14 years: 2 drops once a day.
  • Children over 14 and adults: 4 drops once a day.
  • Dose and age limits may only be changed on a doctor's advice.
  • Shake the vial before use.

Contraindications

  • Individual sensitivity to any ingredient.
  • Hypervitaminosis D, hypercalcemia, hypercalciuria.
  • Renal failure, kidney stone disease.
  • Active form of pulmonary tuberculosis.

Storage conditions

At a temperature not above 25 °C, in a dry place protected from light and out of reach of children.
