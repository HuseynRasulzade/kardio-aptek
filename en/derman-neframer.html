Neframer

Hypertonic sea-water nasal spray — 50 ml.

📞 Order: +994 50 277 94 34

Medical device

Neframer

Intended for the hygiene of the nasal cavity.

Dosage formSpray solution
Packaging50 ml glass vial, cardboard box
Pharmacy dispensingOver the counter
Shelf life3 years

About the product

The label says "not a medicine, a hygiene product"; the back of the box also states "medical device".

Areas of use

  • Reducing nasal congestion
  • Moisturising the nasal mucosa
  • Mechanical cleansing of dust, allergens, microbes and other impurities
  • Nasal hygiene during colds and flu
  • Protection from environmental effects (dry air, air conditioning, dusty environments)

Ingredients

Composition
IngredientAmount
Hypertonic sea water solution2.2 %

Excipients

Deionized water, potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate.

Directions and dosage

  • Shake the vial before use.
  • Use by spraying into the nose.
  • May be applied several times a day.
  • Read the instructions before use.

Contraindications

  • No contraindications are listed on the label — consult a doctor before use.

Storage conditions

At a temperature not above 25 °C, in a dry, cool place out of reach of children.
